Privacy Policy

Bloom · Gym Workouts for Women · Last updated July 28, 2026

Bloom needs some information to build your plan and keep it up to date. This policy says exactly what is stored, why, and how to have it removed. It is written plainly on purpose.

Your account

Bloom signs you in anonymously. We do not ask for your name, your email address, your phone number or a password. Your data is linked to a random identifier created on your device, not to you personally.

What Bloom stores

Your onboarding answers. Whether you use kilograms or pounds, how many days a week you can train, what you want to work on, and the other questions Bloom asks when you start. Bloom needs these to build your plan.

Your training. The plan Bloom generates for you, the sessions you complete, the weights and reps you record, and the exercise swaps and preferences you set. Bloom needs these to decide what you do next week.

How you use the app. Which screens and actions you use, so we can tell which parts of Bloom work and which do not. This is about the app, not about you.

The two period questions

During onboarding Bloom asks when your last period started and your usual gap between periods. These two answers are optional. You can leave them blank and use Bloom normally.

If you answer them, they are stored as part of your profile settings. Bloom is not a period tracker. It does not record individual periods, it does not ask you to log them, and it does not use them for analytics or advertising.

In the current version Bloom stores these answers without changing your training based on them. If that changes in a future version, this page will be updated before the change ships.

We understand this is sensitive information. If you would rather not share it, leave it blank, and if you already answered you can ask us to delete it.

What Bloom does not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not share your training or your period answers with anyone else.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in a database hosted by Supabase, which also handles the anonymous sign-in. Supabase stores and processes the data on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes.

Deleting your data

Email mail@tomerrosenfeld.com and ask us to delete your data. You do not need to give a reason. Please send the request from the device you use Bloom on, or include your Bloom identifier, so we can find the right records. We will delete them and confirm when it is done.

You can also ask us what is stored about you, or ask us to correct it.

Children

Bloom is not intended for children. It is built for adults training in a gym.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. Questions: email mail@tomerrosenfeld.com or open an issue on GitHub.
