Bloom

Gym workout plans for women. Walk in knowing exactly what to do.

Tell Bloom how many days you can train and what you want to work on. It builds a six week plan around your answers, picks your starting weights, and shows you how to do every exercise. Then it keeps up with you: it swaps exercises when a machine is taken, trims the session when you are short on time, and adjusts next week based on how this week went.
